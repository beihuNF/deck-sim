
Major Update #3 - v0.111.1

It's time, Slayers! The third major main branch update is finally here, bringing all of the content, art, balance changes, and bug fixes from v0.108.0-v0.111.0! So if you're a beta branch player, this won't be relevant to you. Go back to Fooling around!

Speaking of which, while the new character and game modes on the current beta branch are the talk of the town right now, all that stuff will need to be tested and polished up quite a bit more before it reaches the main branch. We're not sure when that will be, so if you want to try it out now then switch over to our more experimental beta branch by right-clicking on [i]Slay the Spire 2[/i] in your library and navigating to Properties -> Game Versions & Betas and selecting `public-beta` from the dropdown.

But for those of you seeking a more stable experience, we've got lots of juicy content for you today (4 whole beta patches worth), so let's check it all out!

One of the first things to note is that this update is an extra pretty one! We've introduced some unique VFX for characters' forms, special low-health idle animations, passive Orb VFX, lots of epoch art, and more!

Also... Aeonglass is alive!!! The cool animations that our animator broke down back in the July Neowsletter are finally available for all players, that way everyone can experience the iconic boss in all its glory.

Other major things to note are that Traditional Chinese is now available, keyboard-only mode is an option, there are some new Neow relics, a big batch of character-specific multiplayer cards dropped, and tons of balance changes are here. On with the rest of the notes!
